Organisations

Care organisations form two levels. A vårdgivare (care provider) is the legal entity. Beneath it sit its vårdenheter (care units) — one vårdgivare normally has several. A vårdenhet is the spärrgräns: the boundary a patient's block (spärr) applies across, so which unit a person belongs to is a legal question, not just a label. Affiliations may only target a care organisation or unit. External partners are not part of this hierarchy and are managed in the External Partners panel below.

Registries — Roles & Research Projects

{# #784 E1: the pane said what it technically was, not what it decides. #}

These two lists are the vocabulary the rest of the platform authorises against. They are not free text: an affiliation can only name a role that exists here, and a research read can only name a project that exists here. Adding a row makes a new answer possible across the whole platform; deleting one removes it.

Roles describe what a person is in an organisation — their zone separates care (treating patients) from analysis (secondary use of data), and their permitted phases decide which phase grants make an affiliation complete enough to activate. The role also decides the read purpose the consent gate filters on: the analysis services match on the role's name — one containing "research" reads as research, one containing "quality" or "registr" as quality-registry, anything else clinical as statistics. So the name is load-bearing, not cosmetic: renaming a role can change which patients its holders may see.

Research projects are the units patients consent to, one at a time. Research consent is per project, never blanket: a patient who consented to project A is excluded from project B. The ethics reference belongs with the project because it is the documentation that makes the consent lawful. A project named here with no patient consents yields an empty cohort — which is the correct, and intended, outcome.

Reads are open to professionals; writes are SU-only. Changing a role's permitted phases does not re-run activation for people who already hold that role.
